I am deeply inspired by a future where robots autonomously accomplish real-world tasks, even collaboratively building cities on the Moon. This personal interest has guided my research over the past few years. Drawing on my background and experience in real-time operating systems, on-device robot learning, scheduling theory, and distributed systems principles, I focus on two questions:

  1. How can we advance the design and implementation of operating systems that can be readily deployed on learning-enabled robots, such as Optimus and Figure 01?
  2. How can we leverage real time computing and distributed systems principles to enable robust, time-predictable multi-robot coordination and continual learning from the environment, including through XR-based teleoperation?

If you are interested in pursuing a Ph.D. with me, please email me with your research interests and examples of your work.
